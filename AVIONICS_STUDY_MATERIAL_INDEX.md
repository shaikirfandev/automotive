## Key Engineering Principles Emphasized Throughout

- **Repeatability** — Every test must produce the same result when repeated
- **Determinism** — Software behavior must be predictable and reproducible
- **Traceability** — Every requirement traces to design, code, test, and evidence
- **Independence** — Verification must be independent from development
- **Configuration Control** — Every artifact is version-controlled and baselined
- **Auditability** — All evidence must be auditable by certification authorities
- **Evidence-Based Verification** — Claims require documented proof
- **Defect Prevention** — Processes designed to prevent, not just detect, defects

## Certification Disclaimer

This material is for **educational and interview preparation purposes**. It distinguishes between:
- Regulatory requirements (FAA/EASA mandates)
- Industry guidance (DO-178C objectives)
- Company processes (organization-specific implementations)
- Project-specific certification plans
- Recommended engineering practices

Simplified examples should not be taken as official certification requirements. Always consult the actual DO-178C document and your project's PSAC for authoritative guidance.
